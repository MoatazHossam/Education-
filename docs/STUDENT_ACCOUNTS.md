# Student accounts and the student portal

Added 2026-10-25 on the owner's decision ("add students with school-created accounts"). This is the
first step of the student portal planned with the Wise EduAgent integration
(`docs/wise-integration/`). It gives students a sign-in and shows them their school's curriculum. It
contains **no AI and no Wise features**: those wait for access to Wise EduAgent and a choice of AI
provider (`docs/wise-integration/13_PROJECT_STATUS.md`).

## 1. What it does

| Who | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Platform administrator | Turn student accounts on for a school and set how many (`max_students` limit override on the subscription). Sees only the number of student accounts per school |
| School owner and admins | Add a student, import a roster (CSV), set name, grade and curriculum (one of the school's scopes), issue a new password, remove a student. Page: **Students** (`/org/:orgId/students`) |
| School editors and viewers | Nothing (403) |
| Student | Sign in at `/student-login` with the school code, username and password; **Today** (`/student`), **Homework** (classes and homework, `docs/CLASSROOM.md`) **Tests** (online tests and exams, `docs/ONLINE_TESTS.md`) and **Grades** (their own marks, `docs/CLASSROOM.md` §10); **My books** (`/student/books`, "My curriculum"): the books of their grade in the assigned scope; notifications; switch English/Arabic; change their password; sign out |

A student sees no other student, no staff page, no organization settings and no other school.

## 2. Turning it on (no automatic price or contract change)

- New plan limit `max_students`. It is **0 on every plan**, including existing plans (migration `0007`
  adds `"max_students": 0` to each plan row; prices are unchanged). 0 means off.
- A platform administrator enables it for one school under **Platform admin → Organizations → (school) →
  Student accounts (override)**. The override, like other overrides, applies only while the school is on a
  paid or trial plan; on Free it is ignored.
- When the limit is 0 (never enabled, or the school fell back to Free): staff can still see, edit, reset
  and remove existing students, but cannot add any, and students cannot sign in (`STUDENT_PORTAL_OFF`);
  open student sessions stop working on their next request.
- Pricing for student accounts is a commercial decision still to make; nothing is billed for them
  today. There is no second billing system: the limit lives in the existing plans and overrides.

## 3. Identity

- One sign-in system. A student is a row in `users` with `kind = 'student'` and **no email**; staff
  rows have `kind = 'staff'` and an email (constraint `users_kind_identity`). Students use the same
  Argon2id password hashing, server-side sessions, CSRF protection, lockout and audit as staff.
- Students never hold a membership (trigger `memberships_check_kind`). Every staff route goes through
  `requireUser`/`inOrg`, which refuse student sessions (403 `STAFF_ONLY`); the portal routes go through
  `requireStudentSession`. The kind of an account can never change (trigger `users_kind_immutable`).
- The **school code** is the organization's slug (shown on the Students page, with a sign-in link
  `/student-login?school=<code>`). Usernames are unique per school (3–40 characters: lower-case
  letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `-`); the same username can exist at two schools.
- Passwords are generated by the server (`xxxx-xxxx-xxxx`, 31-letter alphabet without look-alikes,
  about 59 bits), shown **once** to the staff member, and can be downloaded as a CSV to hand out. A
  student may change their own password (at least 10 characters). A staff "New password" replaces it,
  lifts a lockout and signs the student out everywhere.
- Student sessions last at most 12 hours (shared school computers); staff sessions keep their 30 days.
- Sign-in errors do not say whether the school, the username or the password was wrong. Five wrong
  passwords lock the account for 15 minutes; the Students page shows "locked".

## 4. Data

`students` (forced row-level security, `org_id` scoped):

| Column | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `user_id` | The student's `users` row (primary key) |
| `org_id` | The school |
| `username` | Unique per school |
| `grade` | 1–12 (Primary 1 … Secondary 3) |
| `scope_id` | The scope whose books the student sees; must belong to the same school (composite foreign key); deleting the scope leaves the student without a curriculum |
| `created_by`, `created_at`, `updated_at` | |

The student's display name and locale are on `users`. No email, birth date, phone, guardian or
attendance is stored. Since 2026-10-26 a school that uses classes also keeps each student's classes,
homework work (text and files) and marks (`docs/CLASSROOM.md` §6, §9), and their test attempts,
answers, marks and monitoring events (`docs/ONLINE_TESTS.md` §7, §9); deleting the student deletes them.

Deleting a `students` row deletes the account (trigger `students_delete_account`): its sessions and
notifications go with it. Deleting the school deletes all of its students the same way.

## 5. Roster import

CSV with a header row; columns `username`, `name`, `grade`, optional `scope` (a scope name, any case).
Arabic headers (`اسم المستخدم`, `الاسم`, `الصف`, `النطاق`) are accepted; `;` separators and a UTF-8
byte-order mark are handled (Excel); files must be UTF-8. At most 300 students per file. The import is
previewed line by line (invalid usernames, duplicates in the file or at the school, grades outside 1–12,
unknown scopes, empty names) and applied **all or nothing**, inside the plan limit. The response carries
the new passwords once, for the credentials download.

## 6. API

Staff (owner and admins; organization routes, CSRF as usual):

| Method and path | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/orgs/:orgId/students` | `{ enabled, limit, used, schoolCode, grades, students[] }` |
| `POST /api/orgs/:orgId/students` | `{ username, name, grade, scopeId? }` → 201 `{ student, password }` |
| `POST /api/orgs/:orgId/students/import` | `{ csv, apply }` → preview `{ rows, errors, limit, used }` or applied `{ created[] (with passwords), schoolCode }` |
| `PATCH /api/orgs/:orgId/students/:userId` | `{ name?, grade?, scopeId? (null clears) }` |
| `POST /api/orgs/:orgId/students/:userId/reset-password` | → `{ password }`; ends the student's sessions |
| `DELETE /api/orgs/:orgId/students/:userId` | Deletes the account |

Students:

| Method and path | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /api/auth/student-login` | `{ school, username, password }` |
| `GET /api/auth/me` | For a student: `user` (`kind: "student"`, `email: null`) and `student { username, grade, school { id, name, code, locale } }` |
| `GET /api/student/curriculum` | `{ school, scope { id, name, academicYear } \| null, grade, subjects[] }`: the subjects of the student's grade with only the books selected in the scope |
| `PATCH /api/student/profile` | `{ locale }` |
| `POST /api/student/password` | `{ currentPassword, newPassword }`; signs out other devices |
| `POST /api/auth/logout` | As for staff |

Errors: `STUDENTS_NOT_ENABLED` (402), `PLAN_LIMIT_REACHED` (402), `USERNAME_TAKEN` (409),
`IMPORT_INVALID` (400), `STAFF_ONLY` (403), `STUDENT_PORTAL_OFF` (403), `ORG_SUSPENDED` (403),
`INVALID_CREDENTIALS` (401).

## 7. Audit

In the school's audit log: `student.created`, `student.imported` (count), `student.updated` (changed
field names), `student.password_reset`, `student.removed`, `student.password_changed`,
`auth.student_login`, `auth.student_login_failed`, `auth.student_locked`,
`auth.student_login_blocked_locked`. Entries carry the username, never a password or the student's name.

## 8. Tests

- `tests/integration/students.test.ts` (25 tests): off by default and on per school; limit and the
  Free-plan fallback; sign-in, equal errors, lockout and reset; password change; **every route of the
  application called with a student session** (only the portal routes succeed; staff routes answer
  `STAFF_ONLY`); staff accounts cannot use the portal; database triggers and constraints;
  editors/viewers refused; another school gets 404 and sees nothing under RLS; scopes of another school
  refused; removal and school deletion delete the accounts; platform user list excludes students;
  import preview, all-or-nothing apply, Excel/Arabic files, limit; the portal shows only the grade's
  books in the assigned scope; suspended school; generated passwords.
- `tests/e2e/journeys.spec.ts`: a platform admin turns students on, the owner adds one and imports a
  roster (with the credentials download), the student signs in from the staff sign-in page's link, sees
  exactly the scope's books for their grade, cannot open staff pages, switches to Arabic, and is signed
  out by a staff password reset.
- `tests/e2e/accessibility.spec.ts`: the Students page and its three dialogs, the student sign-in, and
  the portal in English, Arabic, dark and on a phone (axe WCAG 2.2 AA, 0 findings).

## 9. Open points

1. **Legal assessment before real students use it** (SECURITY_DESIGN §7): children's data under the
   Egyptian PDPL, parental consent, the school as controller, retention, privacy notice.
2. Price of student accounts (currently enabled only by a platform administrator, free of charge).
3. Server validation messages in the import preview are in English (as in the catalogue sheet import);
   the rest of the student pages are translated.
4. Many students usually share one school address (the school's NAT). Since 2026-10-27 the limits on
   what students do in the portal (starting a test, saving answers, heartbeats, page events, homework
   drafts, hand-ins and uploads, password changes) count per student, and the flood guard allows 6,000
   requests a minute per address for signed-in requests while holding each account to 600. Before,
   these counted per address: a whole school shared, for example, 60 test starts in 15 minutes and
   1,200 heartbeats an hour, which one class of 30 used up in about 13 minutes of an exam. Measured
   with `scripts/load-exam.mjs` (TEST_REPORT §5).
5. Students have no self-service data export or account deletion: the school, expected to be the
   controller, sees every stored field on the Students page and removes accounts. Confirm this with
   the legal assessment.
6. Wise EduAgent features (assistant, practice, progress…) are not built: Wise could not be inspected
   and no AI provider is chosen. Classes, homework and online tests (not from Wise) are built:
   `docs/CLASSROOM.md`, `docs/ONLINE_TESTS.md`.
