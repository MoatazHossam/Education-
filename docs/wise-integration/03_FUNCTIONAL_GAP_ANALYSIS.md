# Functional gap analysis

The gap matrix, the foundations needed whatever Wise contains, and the decisions needed from the owner
are in [`docs/WISE_INTEGRATION_GAP_ANALYSIS.md`](../WISE_INTEGRATION_GAP_ANALYSIS.md). It is kept in one
place to avoid two diverging copies.

Summary (2026-10-25): every Wise column is NOT ACCESSIBLE. The SaaS can reuse identity, tenancy,
entitlements, metering, audit, alerts, localization and accessibility testing, and offers the school's
content scopes as curriculum context. It lacks students, enrolment, content, assessment, progress and AI.
