# Target audience

Who the video is for, what each audience needs to see, and what it should do next. The segments match
chapter 3 of the 20-minute cut (`c3-segments`) and `docs/COMMERCIAL_FEASIBILITY.md`. No market figures are
quoted in the video: there are no measured ones yet, and the pilot is where value will be measured.

## Primary viewers

| Audience | Their question | What the video shows them | What we ask of them |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Owners, principals and academic directors of private, language and international schools; school groups** (Egypt first) | Can one place hold what we teach in every grade and campus, and the daily work of teachers and students? | Shared content scopes, comparison between campuses and years, notes and assignees, classes, homework, online tests with monitoring, gradebook, student portal; roles, audit, data kept apart per school; Arabic first | Join the pilot as a partner school |
| **EdTech companies, content producers and publishers** | Which part of the curriculum do we cover this year, and can our systems read it? | A documented scope per academic year, verification marks with sources, yearly catalogue updates and carry-forward, read API with keys, signed webhooks | A pilot integration with their content pipeline |
| **Tutoring centres and curriculum consultants** | Can I prepare clear coverage plans, compare them and hand them over? | Scopes, comparison, exports (text, CSV, JSON), notes per book | Try the free plan; join the pilot |

## Secondary viewers

| Audience | Their question | What the video shows them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Investors and strategic partners** | Is this a real product with a sound model, and what is honestly left? | A working product end to end (45 % of the 20-minute cut is the running application), multi-tenancy enforced in the database, subscriptions with limits, central updates, operations drills, and a plain list of what remains before launch |
| **IT and data-protection reviewers at schools** | Is children's data safe, and who can see it? | School-created accounts with no student e-mail, row-level isolation, two-step verification, support access only by the school's time-boxed permission, virus checks and photo metadata removal, retention periods; legal review stated as pending |
| **Teachers** | Will this save me work, and do I keep the final say? | Homework and tests linked to the curriculum, automatic marking only by fixed rules, written answers marked by the teacher, live monitoring without cameras, the gradebook |
| **Students and parents** (indirect) | Is it simple, and is it fair? | One portal for the school day, on a phone, in Arabic or English; saved answers on weak connections; what the teacher sees during a test |

## Not the audience today

- **Universities and higher education**: named in the video as a later expansion opportunity, not as
  supported (no credit hours or course registration; the catalogue is pre-university).
- **Parents as account holders**: parent accounts are on the roadmap, not built.
- **Buyers looking for an AI tutor now**: the assistant is shown only as future vision; the video says it is
  not built.

## Tone for these viewers

Confident, calm and precise; Modern Standard Arabic that an Egyptian, Gulf or Levantine school leader reads
naturally; English subtitles for regional and international partners. Status is always visible: a viewer
should never have to guess whether something exists today.
